practical information 

Where is Terramadas?
Between Castelo Branco and Fundao in Central Portugal.

How do I reach Terramadas?
The easiest and fastest way is to fly to Lisbon airport. You can choose to rent a car at the airport or rent a car in Castelo Branco (cheeper option). 
To reach Castelo Branco by public transport:
At Lisbon airport take a taxi or metro (red line - 3 stops) to Oriente station. There you take a train (https://cp.pt/en) or bus (https://rede-expressos.pt/en) to Castelo Branco.
Another option is to take a taxi to Sete Rios busstation where you can take a bus to Castelo Branco. (https://rede-expressos.pt/en or https://www.flixbus.pt/)
In Castelo Branco you can rent a car at Guerin or Europcar.
Terramadas is about half an hour north of Castelo Branco.

You can also fly to Porto and take a bus to Castelo Branco - a bit longer on the bus but the flights are sometimes cheaper. (https://rede-expressos.pt/en and  https://www.flixbus.pt/)

Do I need a car to reach Terramadas?
We recommend you to be independent. This will also allow you to visit the area. 
However you can also take a taxi at the bus/train station of Castelo Branco (price is about 40 euro)
By public bus: line 102 leaves from the bus/trainstation several times a day. There are no busses on Saturday and Sunday and festive days. The trip will take about 1 hour. Please download the Mobicab app to consult up to date schedules!!!

Can I reach the location with a normal car?
The village can easily be reached with a normal car. Parking space is nearby.
The farm is not reachable by normal car, a 4x4 is needed since the road got damaged by the storms last winter. This means you will need to park your car in the village and enjoy a beautiful 15 min walk to reach us. 

What is a good season to visit Terramadas?
The weather will most likely be good between Easter and mid November (don't take it as a promise). Although weather-permitting you are welcome all year round.
​Note that summers (mid June till mid September are hot and dry = 35º C). However there are many river beaches in the area where you can cool down. Being at an altitude, the evenings and nights are generally cool.
​
Can I stay at Terramadas?
Our guesthouse is not finished yet. Subscribe to the newsletter to stay updated and book your stay.
In the meantime you can stay in nearby guesthouses. contact us for a list of recommendations.

​Can we volunteer?
Yes, once the guest-studio is finished we will be able to host volunteers. Please contact us to share your interest.
