About us

JOERI
What I appreciated most in my studies of architecture at sint Lukas in Ghent is their continuous invitation to think out of the box. It was not just drawing houses but designing futuristic projects, giving a structural image to an idea or a philosophy. I learned to approach architecture not just as a set of measures that must be calculated, but as the translation of a vision into matter.

However after graduating I worked for a big industrial construction company, first as a building site manager, later as an architect and eventually I got to apply my second passion and restructured their IT department. In the mean time I started designing within Arduino and made things work that were thought to be impossible. Let’s say I got a 6th sense for efficiency. I love to get a task in front of me where I have to figure out how it works and how to get the most out of it. 

When I started working for a school of self-development and meditation I gave myself the task to figure out how to make them into a thriving business. It was an interesting journey and I learned a lot about myself and about the nature of doing business. What attracts people? When does an event work and when not? Which role does our inner state plays into doing business? I left when I felt I had brought all structures to a place of efficiency and they could go forward. Once that job is done, I feel ready for a new challenge. 

Moving to Portugal and building our off-grid homestead was my next challenge! I get at my best when I need to work with a series of restrictions. An old ruin with thick granite stone walls limiting the possibilities, is a good exercise in Tetris. It requires creativity to not just fill the space in a clumsy way, but to transform the space into shapes that express our being and fold around our daily movements. Being off-grid is excellent exercise in re-thinking our way of using resources. It requires so many aspects of our day-to-day life to be rethought and these are exactly the things I like to play with. Our personal situation forced us to have to consider so much more aspects than one would think about due to the climate. We can’t just repeat the life we had in modern society and mimic their systems to fulfill all our ‘needs’. We investigated our needs, weighting them against responsibility and comfort, but without loosing style. The good news all are possible at the same time! 

So, whatever project I get in front of me, be it a house, a business, a personal goal, I like to dissect it, following each tread as a wire into the greater picture. Analyzing it with the human inside. To give you a roadmap that reflects your dream in a realistic way.

KATHLIEN
During my final year at the Academy of fine Arts in Venice I painted people in meditation or prayer under the title: "Sguardi oltre l'orrizonte": "Glances Beyond the Horizon". Like me, they were searching for what lies beyond our horizon-skin, beyond our tangible reality. I looked for the invisible, the intangible, the deeper meaning to life.

After 7 years in Italy and many exotic travels I moved back to Belgium. My son Ilai was born and opened me up to the amazing experience of becoming a mother. Birth revealed to be the most powerful experience of my life and I got passionate about it. I trained as a doula and cranio-sacral therapist and in 2015, I founded Innata. Guiding children into this world became my great passion. As a doula and “spiritual midwife,” I supported babies and women through childbirth. 

With the years my work expanded. We do not get born only once. Many times as an adult we long to be reborn, to find ourselves again beyond society conformisms. Through the bodywork, meditation, and creative coaching, I could guide people in feeling and discovering who they truly are. They started to hear the voice of their heart again. They began to honor themselves.

I was lucky to be able to work with people from all over the world, to get to know their traditions, to feel their deep roots and to discover the many expressions of humans on earth. 

But Belgium wasn’t my home. I suffered living in a busy society and craved for nature, and I didn’t seem to see the way how to fulfill that dream. The answer was an intens training in spiritual constellations.  I experienced the power of the spiritual constellation work first hand. I could release the loyalties that were holding me back and heal the old trauma. I could lift my anchors while developing deep compassion for the human journey. In the mean time I have accompanied many to release their inner blocks, heal the pain of the ancestors and transform the patterns of sufferings we unknowingly carry. 

The reward of the work was Portugal. 15 years of holding the vision acknowledged with a place that finally feels like home. But this doesn’t feel like the endpoint, rather a beginning of a whole new part of my life. Transforming Terramadas from wilderness to a home hasn't been different than digging for the gold in the mud of what a human can experience. 
I keep the space open for our ancestors, for the soul, and for Spirit, so that a new future becomes possible through a new clarity and expanded understanding. So that you, I, and we can experience together what it means to be truly fulfilled human beings.​
