Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026
Who we are. Terramadas, in Central Portugal (between Castelo Branco and Fundao). You can reach us at info@terramadas.org.
What this website does not do. Our pages load no scripts, fonts, captchas, analytics or advertising trackers from other websites. Nothing is sent to anyone else just because you visit a page.
Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). As with any web host, the host receives technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests a page. We do not use this data.
Newsletter. If you subscribe, your email address is sent to MailerLite, the service we use to store subscribers and send the newsletter. This happens only when you tick the box and press Subscribe. We use your address only to send you news about tree planting, the magazine and events. The legal basis is your consent. We keep your address until you unsubscribe, which you can do at any time with the link in every newsletter.
Contact. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you.
Links to other websites. Some links lead to other services, such as TidyCal (booking), Buy Me a Coffee and public transport websites. They receive nothing from you unless you click, and their own privacy policies apply from then on.
Your rights. You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, or withdraw your consent at any time by writing to info@terramadas.org. You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the CNPD (cnpd.pt).
