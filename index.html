​Bom dia,
​
We are Kathlien and Joeri, the guardians of a 6ha (15 acres) property in Central Portugal consisting of large, fertile terraces with multiple springs and mountain slopes covered with majestic boulders and ancient memories. The land is home to our family and animals where we aim to live self-responsible and together-sufficient in terms of resources and food. We chose this mountain as the perfect home base for our journey of self-discovery in the many layers of existence aided by the mirror of Mother Earth.

Our Story

We are here for the lover of the Earth, the children that wish to feel small again in Her embrace.​
It all started with a dream. 
A dream to contribute to the healing of our beloved Earth. 
A dream to create a self-sufficient little paradise for humans to live in harmony with nature. 
A dream to treat it as a temple, a place to remember we are but children in the arms of something Greater. 

And so it went. In 2024 we found it. We fell in love with it at first sight. This was it, our new Home. An abandoned and bewildered farm in a moonlike landscape. 

But 3 weeks before we were supposed to move in, the largest wildfire ever recorded in Portugal came our way.
