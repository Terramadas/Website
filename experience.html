Terramadas

We are the guardians of a 6ha (15 acres) property consisting of 1ha large, fertile terraces with multiple springs and 5ha of mountain slopes covered with majestic boulders and ancient memories. The land is home to our family and animals where we aim to live self-responsible and together-sufficient in terms of resources and food. We chose this mountain as the perfect home base for our journey of self-discovery in the many layers of existence aided by the mirror of Mother Earth. 
​
Our vision is to showcase and inspire a self-responsible way of living in both a practical and spiritual reality. A refuge where we can remember the divine potential of the Human being rooted in humbleness, simplicity and nurtured by the care for each other and the Earth.

plug in off-grid

Off-grid and design: introduction  - live and online - by Joeri 
Gain more knowledge on off grid systems for power, water and black and grey waste water treatment, wormtoilet and heating + how to build them. Learn about our gardens and our planting and composting techniques inspired by permaculture, natural farming and local farmers wisdom. Learn about the challenges and blessings of this climate, environment and lifestyle and the solutions we have come up with to be prepared for whatever life brings. Ask your questions and hear our story of resilience and regeneration.
​
In depth off-grid and design: personalized  - live and online - by Joeri
For those who have attended the introduction or already have a basic knowledge on off-grid living.
In this meeting we focus on the area of your request so you can go home with new insights and a sense of overview on how to proceed in your own project. We can give advise on the finetuning of the design of your (semi of full off-grid) property and zoom in on the needed steps and phases on how to create your project financially and practically.
Topics can be: solar power setup and calculations, rocket stove combined central heating, grey water purification systems, humanure and vermicomposting toilets, grey water reuse, programming with Arduino and Linux, designing in Sketchup,...
*may require more than one session...

immersion

Meditation - only live - by Kathlien
There is a realm of sensations, information and revelation beyond that what is seen. Meditation offers a way to deepen our experience of ourselves and reality. Meditating in nature or at specific power places add interaction between us and the wisdom of the place itself. We embark on these journeys setting intentions, asking questions and being open to receive the unknown. 
Choose between:
  • Pilgrimage to and meditation at a local spring known for its healing waters (easy flat terrain)
  • Guided meditation at the 3 springs; ancestor stones or snakehead-cave at Terramadas
    ​
Herbs - only live - by Kathlien
There is something timeless and fairytale-like to walk around and pick what nature shares in an effortless abundance. So much of the green we label as weeds is actually edible to humans. Join me on a walk through Terramadas looking for wild herbs, learn how to recognize and use them. We will learn about their wild wisdom with all of our senses and pick what is available to make a hydrolat (floral water) and/or salve, salads, thee's,...

From farm to table - Plantbased cooking - only live - by Kathlien
When envisioning to live as self-sufficient as possible, there are some habits to reshape. This session focusses on living with what the land gives being fully nurtured and without feeling deprived. We will prepare tasty and healthy salads, soups, stews, deserts, snacks,... with basic ingredients from the season that you can easily grow yourself (without gluten, animal milk, soy or oil.) - according to your taste and wishes.
