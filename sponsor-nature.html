Buy nature a tree

Your gift will allow us to plant more trees and reforest the mountain providing ecological harmony, food, shade and shelter.

65417 hectares burned in August 2025 in the biggest wildfire of Portuguese that lasted for 11 days. The intensity was unseen and the flames were extremely violent pushed by a strong wind. It left the mountain covered with the remains of burned bushes and trees. 
Our vision is to bring the forest back to the mountain, doing our part of the effort.
We have been cutting down the burned remains to use them to build retention dams. These dams will prevent erosion and hold an area of soil around the tree. As the area is also home to wild boar and goats, young trees also need netting for protection.
Your donations will allow us to not only to buy seeds and plant more trees but also to invest in the needed materials such as waterpumps, water tanks, irrigation and labour to keep these trees alive and thriving.

and contribute to the reforestation of this magical mountain.

Which tree's?

  • Around the house : fruit trees (our private investment)
  • On the mountainslopes: native trees such as chestnut, cork-oak, fig, olive, myrtle, medronho, carob,...
  • Near seasonal springs and seasonal creeks: elderberry and willow.
  • Planned experiment: sterile Paulownia (elongata x fortunei), a fast growing tree which is said to be very fire resistant and would allow us to grow a shade giving canopy to protect the younger trees in little time. 
We are buying most of them about 1 to 1,5m of height to give us a head start, but we are also growing trees ourselves in our little nursery. ​

Progress

  • Sept '25: Barbara (Mount of oaks) and Fransisco teach us how to make retention dams on the contour lines. We also follow a workshop with Jake Corin on soil microbiology and ecology.
  • Sept '25: A group of volunteers joins for a day to make the first retention dams on the mountain slope closest to the house.
  • Sept '25: We create our first wormfarm. Their castings will help to fertilize the trees.
  • Oct-Nov '25: We spread seeds of oat, barley, pulses, clover (inoculated with mycorrhizae fungi) all over the area. Their growth will help to fix the soil. We also collect a lot of charcoal to soak in EM water (=charged biochar).
  • Oct '25: A digger comes and makes paths through the burned bush so we have a first road of access
  • Nov '25: We take down eucalyptus regrowth 2 times. (But it still grew back. We will need the help of goats here?) Eucalyptus create fire tornados and spread with fire, we would like to replace them with a friendlier species.
  • Oct '25-June '26: We take down more burned bush to clear the terraces around the house and mulch the remains. The mulch will be composted and used as nutrition around the trees.
  • March '26: We plant over 30 trees and bushes in our private area closest to the house.​
