Terramadas Magazine

During those years of longing for a different kind of life, we have traveled and met people and places who showed us it was possible, they were living their dream, one we were silently cherishing too. Seeing those real life examples has been crucial for us.
At the same time we have dug into the constrictions of our own limitations, we grew our awareness, healed wounds and gained inner freedom.
In the same way, we want to be an inspiration and give courage to those who wish to contribute to life on this planet in a meaningful way - whatever that might be for you! We wish to inspire others to envision and allow a new future for themselves. A future that is nourishing to you, others and the planet. 
We figured out that creating this platform, sharing our story, nurturing others with our journey and art is a way to do so.

​We will not share our writing on social media as we all receive an overload of stories on a daily basis, most of them aiming to manipulate us in some way or another. I wish to write for the ones who not just want to consume another story but choose to taste it. ​​
​
Sharing our journey to inspire your heart to live a true life.
